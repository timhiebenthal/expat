What should the monthly counts do when the household is a couple?

The counts you set per Cost item are household totals: open the analysis as a couple and cappuccinos reads 8, because 4 a month is a personal habit and there are two of you. The question this demo exists to settle is what happens to those numbers when the household size changes, and whether the “per Member” label underneath each one is enough to keep them honest.

Two rules were on the table and you can still flip between them, though the demo now opens on Immediately, the one worth building. Immediately - untouched counts follow the household straight away. When the analysis is re-opened - the counts only follow the household when the analysis is derived again, so changing the household size alone moves nothing. Your own edits keep their value under both rules; the difference is only ever in the ones you never touched.

Press buttons in any order, or work through the three scenarios at the bottom. The awkward moments to look for are when a number stops meaning what you thought it meant, and when a household change appears to do nothing.
